RFC 1035 limits a single DNS label to 63 octets or less.
RFC 1035 limits a complete domain name to 512 octets or less.
The authors of RFC 1035 picked the 63-octet label limit because of a memory constraint in the PDP-10 machines at ISI in 1987.
The 255-octet domain name limit in RFC 1035 is too small for modern applications and ought to be raised.
